About Smart Campus Navigation

A web-based campus directory, facility finder, and floor-wise navigation solution (PRD v1.0)

🏛️ 6-Level Building Directory

The academic building contains 300 numbered rooms across 6 levels (50 rooms per floor):

  • Ground Floor (1–50): Reception, Director, Admin, Computer Labs 1–2, Main Mess, Central Library, Medical Room.
  • First Floor (101–150): Classrooms 101–110, CSE Department, Robotics & IoT Labs.
  • Second Floor (201–250): Classrooms 201–210, AI & ML Labs, Data Analytics, HOD AI&ML.
  • Third Floor (301–350): Classrooms 301–310, IT Department, Operating Systems & Full-Stack Labs.
  • Fourth Floor (401–450): Classrooms 401–410, Cyber Security, Blockchain, Cloud Labs.
  • Fifth Floor (501–550): Central Seminar Hall, Auditorium, Startup Incubation Centre.

🔗 Shared Facilities & Circulation

Key building-wide facilities connecting all levels:

  • Twin Lifts (Lifts 1 & 2): Central lift lobby serving Ground through 5th Floor.
  • Staircase Cores: Main circulation stairs plus east/west emergency escape stairs.
  • Restrooms: Men's, Women's, and Wheelchair-Accessible washrooms on every floor.
