Project Overview

About The Campus Atlas

A fast, static spatial guide built to help students, staff and guests reach university destinations quickly without confusion.

Design Concept: The Printed Campus Atlas

The Smart Campus Navigation System was designed around a distinct philosophy: bringing the elegance and clarity of a well-made printed atlas to an interactive web experience.

Instead of generic AI templates, glossy glass gradients or neon shadows, the site is grounded in calm paper tones (#F6F2E9), deep pine green (#0E4B41), and a single high-contrast signal orange (#E8552D) reserved exclusively for navigation routes and principal actions.

Honest Data Policy

Verified vs. Sample Data

In accordance with the project specification (PRD v2.0 Section 2 and Section 13), sample data is never presented as factual. Every location record is explicitly categorized:

Verified by campus office

Coordinates, room designations, operating hours, and entrance doors have been confirmed directly on-site by university campus facilities staff.

Sample data, not verified

Populated for testing, demonstration, or structural modeling. Unverified details (such as hours or specific phone numbers) are deliberately omitted rather than guessed.

To replace sample data with production data, campus administrators can simply update the static data/locations.json schema and mark locations as "verified": true.

Dijkstra Shortest Path Routing

Navigation directions are calculated mathematically in the browser using Dijkstra's algorithm running across a weighted graph network of campus nodes and walkable paths.

  • Walkable Edge Weights: Measured in real meters along paved walkways.
  • Estimated Walk Time: Computed at a standard calibrated walking speed of 80 meters per minute (4.8 km/h).
  • Step-Free Accessibility: Walkway segments feature an accessible: true/false flag to support ramp and elevator-only navigation.
